Stillglyph Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Stillglyph, localized as 静页 in Simplified Chinese, is a private, local-first reflection app developed by xinbao gan. This policy explains how version 1.0 handles information.

Information stored on your device

Stillglyph may store readings, questions, journal notes, favorites, personal card connections, custom spreads, study progress, practice history, weekly summaries, reminder preferences, and unfinished ritual state in the app's local container on your device.

This content is used to provide the features you choose inside the app. Stillglyph does not require an account and does not send this content to a developer-operated server.

No developer data collection

Stillglyph does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated backend. The developer does not collect, sell, rent, or use your readings and journal content for advertising or profiling.

Optional camera access

If you enable Air Ritual, Stillglyph asks for camera permission so Apple's Vision framework can recognize supported hand gestures. Camera frames are analyzed in memory on your device. Stillglyph does not record, save, or upload camera images or video.

Camera access is optional. Touch controls remain available, and you can revoke permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Notifications and system shortcuts

If you enable a daily reflection reminder, Stillglyph schedules a local notification on your device. It does not use a push-notification server. The optional Today's Card shortcut asks iOS to open the corresponding in-app screen; it does not send your reading content to the developer.

Exports and sharing

When you choose to export or share content, Stillglyph prepares a temporary file on your device and presents Apple's share sheet. During normal sharing, the managed temporary copy is removed when the share flow closes. If sharing is interrupted before cleanup finishes, Stillglyph attempts cleanup again on a later app launch and when Settings is opened.

Copies that you intentionally save to Files, email, messaging apps, or another destination are controlled by you and that destination. They remain there until you delete them.

Apple device backups

Depending on your Apple account and device settings, iOS may include app data in an Apple-managed device backup. Apple, not Stillglyph, provides and controls that service. Stillglyph does not operate a separate cloud backup or cross-device synchronization service.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

  • You can delete individual saved content or use the relevant reset and delete controls in Settings.
  • You can revoke camera and notification permissions in iPhone Settings.
  • Removing the app can remove its local container, subject to any backup retained under your Apple account settings.
  • Because Stillglyph does not maintain an account or developer server containing your journal data, there is no server-side account data for the developer to retrieve or delete.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated before a release materially changes how information is collected, used, stored, or shared. The effective date above identifies the current version.

隐私政策

静页隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 27 日

静页（英文名 Stillglyph）是由 xinbao gan 开发的本地优先私人反思 App。本政策说明 1.0 版本如何处理信息。

保存在设备上的信息

静页可能会把解读、问题、日记、收藏、个人牌义联结、自定义牌阵、学习进度、练习历史、每周总结、提醒偏好和未完成仪式保存在你设备上的 App 本地空间中。

这些内容只用于提供你在 App 内主动使用的功能。静页不要求账号，也不会把这些内容发送到开发者运营的服务器。

开发者不收集数据

静页不包含广告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK、跨 App 跟踪或开发者运营的后端。开发者不会收集、出售、出租你的解读和日记内容，也不会将其用于广告或用户画像。

可选的相机权限

如果你启用 Air Ritual，静页会请求相机权限，并使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架识别支持的手势。相机画面只在设备内存中分析；静页不会录制、保存或上传相机图像或视频。

相机不是必需权限。你仍可使用触控操作，也可以随时在 iPhone“设置”中撤销权限。

通知与系统快捷指令

如果你启用每日反思提醒，静页会在设备上安排本地通知，不使用推送通知服务器。可选的“今日之牌”快捷指令只会请求 iOS 打开对应的 App 页面，不会把你的解读内容发送给开发者。

导出与分享

当你主动导出或分享内容时，静页会在设备上准备临时文件，并打开 Apple 系统分享面板。正常分享结束后，App 会删除受管的临时副本；如果分享在清理完成前被中断，静页会在之后启动 App 以及打开“设置”时再次尝试清理。

你主动保存到“文件”、邮件、消息 App 或其他位置的副本由你和对应目的地管理，并会一直保留到你自行删除。

Apple 设备备份

根据你的 Apple 账号与设备设置，iOS 可能会把 App 数据纳入 Apple 管理的设备备份。该服务由 Apple 提供和管理，不属于静页服务。静页没有独立的云端备份或跨设备同步服务。

保留、删除与用户选择

  • 你可以删除单条已保存内容，或使用“设置”中的对应重置与删除功能。
  • 你可以在 iPhone“设置”中撤销相机和通知权限。
  • 删除 App 可能会移除其本地空间，但仍需考虑 Apple 账号设置中保留的设备备份。
  • 静页没有保存你日记数据的开发者账号或服务器，因此开发者没有可供调取或删除的服务器端账号数据。

政策变更

如果未来版本实质改变信息的收集、使用、保存或分享方式，本政策会在该版本发布前更新。页面顶部的生效日期代表当前版本。
