When contacting support, include the app version, iPhone model, iOS version, what you were trying to do, and the steps that led to the issue. Before attaching a screenshot, hide any private questions, cards, journal notes, feeling words, or exported content.

Local data and recovery

Stillglyph is local-first and does not provide an account or developer-operated cloud synchronization. An unfinished ritual can normally be resumed or discarded from the Ritual tab. Saved readings and notes remain in the app unless you delete them, reset the relevant feature, remove the app, or restore the device from a different state.

Exports are readable copies, not a supported import-and-restore backup. iOS device backups are provided and controlled by Apple according to your Apple account settings.

Camera and Air Ritual

Camera access is optional and is used only for on-device hand-pose recognition. If access was denied, open iPhone Settings, find Stillglyph, and enable Camera. Touch controls remain available if you prefer not to use the camera.

For Air Ritual, complete the required shuffles, move the light over the deck, then make a fist and hold until the progress ring completes. Individual card reveal may use the gesture shown in the current in-app guide.

Local reminders

Daily reflection reminders are optional local notifications. Enable or change them in Stillglyph Settings. If permission was denied, use the app's Open Settings action or open iPhone Settings and allow notifications for Stillglyph.

Exports and deletion

Use the export controls in Settings to create a copy for sharing. Stillglyph attempts to remove its managed temporary copy after sharing and retries cleanup after an interrupted share. Files you save elsewhere remain under your control.

Use the relevant delete or reset controls in Settings to remove local content. Destructive actions require confirmation. Removing the app can remove its local data, subject to any Apple-managed device backup.

Accessibility

Stillglyph supports Dynamic Type, VoiceOver labels, Reduce Motion, and touch alternatives to camera gestures. If a control is difficult to reach or understand, contact us with the screen name, text size, accessibility settings, and steps to reproduce the issue.

Safety and scope

Stillglyph is a private reflection and entertainment experience. It does not provide medical, mental-health, legal, or financial advice and should not be used for emergency or safety-critical decisions.

本地数据与恢复

静页采用本地优先设计，没有账号或开发者运营的云同步。未完成的仪式通常可以从“仪式”页恢复或放弃。已保存的解读和笔记会留在 App 中，除非你主动删除、重置对应功能、删除 App，或把设备恢复到其他状态。

导出文件是可阅读副本，不是当前支持重新导入 App 的恢复备份。iOS 设备备份由 Apple 根据你的 Apple 账号设置提供和管理。

相机与 Air Ritual

相机权限是可选的，只用于设备上的手势识别。如果之前拒绝权限，请打开 iPhone“设置”，找到静页并允许相机。即使不使用相机，仍可使用触控操作。

使用 Air Ritual 时，请先完成要求的洗牌，把光点移到牌组上，然后握拳并保持，直到进度环完成。单张牌的翻牌手势请以当前 App 内指南为准。

本地提醒

每日反思提醒是可选的本地通知，可在静页“设置”中开启或调整。如果之前拒绝过权限，请使用 App 内的“打开设置”操作，或前往 iPhone“设置”为静页开启通知。

导出与删除

你可以在“设置”中创建导出副本并分享。静页会在分享结束后尝试删除受管的临时副本；如果分享被中断，之后还会再次尝试清理。你保存到其他位置的文件仍由你管理。

使用“设置”中的对应删除或重置功能可以移除本地内容。破坏性操作会要求确认。删除 App 可能会移除本地数据，但仍需考虑 Apple 管理的设备备份。

辅助功能

静页支持动态字体、VoiceOver 标签、减弱动态效果以及相机手势的触控替代。如果某个控件难以触及或理解，请在联系时提供页面名称、文字大小、辅助功能设置和复现步骤。

安全与用途边界

静页是一款私人反思与娱乐体验，不提供医疗、心理健康、法律或财务建议，也不应被用于紧急或安全关键决策。
